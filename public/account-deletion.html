SPEAXA Account & Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Store & Apple App Store developer requirements and our Privacy Policy, users have full control to permanently delete their SPEAXA account (Teacher, Student, or Parent) and all associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

Users can permanently delete their SPEAXA account directly from within the mobile application under Settings / Profile → Delete Account, or by submitting a deletion request using the online form below.

In-App Path: Profile Settings Delete Account Confirm & Delete

Permanent Deletion Notice

Account deletion is irreversible. Once processed, your profile, authentication credentials, course access, wallet balances, progress reports, certificates, and messages will be permanently purged from our servers.

What data will be deleted?
  • Account Details: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, Profile Picture, and Password Hash.
  • Academic Data: Enrolled Batches, Live Class Attendance, Test & Poll Responses, and Certificates.
  • Submissions & Activity: Homework/Assignment Submissions, Teacher Observations, and Classroom Chat History.
  • Financial & Governance: Wallet Ledger, Payout Requests, Rewards, Teacher SOP Submissions, and Push Notification Tokens.

Deletion Processing Timeframe

In-App / Password Verified: Instant permanent deletion upon confirmation.
Web Deletion Request: Verified and processed by our compliance team within 24 to 48 hours.

Submit Online Account Deletion Request
Providing your valid password allows the system to verify your identity and delete your account instantly.
